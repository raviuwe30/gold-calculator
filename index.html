Gold price calculator – FAQ

How do I calculate the 22K gold price from the 24K rate?

Multiply the 24K rate by 0.916 (91.6% purity, hallmark 916). If 24K gold is ₹1,13,250 per 10 grams, 22K gold is about ₹1,03,737 per 10 grams.

How do I calculate the 18K gold price?

Multiply the 24K rate by 0.75 (75% purity, hallmark 750).

How is the gold price per gram calculated?

Take the 10-gram price for your purity, add or deduct your percentage, divide by 10 for the rate per gram, then multiply by the weight in grams (milligrams ÷ 1000).

Does it include making charges or GST?

No. It shows the gold value only. Use the percentage field to add or deduct a margin.
